# Certifications for the AI Solution Engineer

Free credentials, paid certificate programs and proctored exams worth considering, with facts collected from official pages on **6 November 2026**. Programs change often: always confirm on the official page before you start or book. `Unknown` means the fact was not published or could not be confirmed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Prepare honestly. Brain dumps and having someone or something take a course, quiz or exam for you violate every vendor's candidate agreement and lead to revoked credentials and bans.

## Recommended path

| Stage | Goal | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Free credentials | Prove you can build with current APIs | Anthropic Academy (*Building with the Claude API*, *Introduction to Model Context Protocol*), OpenAI Academy (*Agents and Workflows*), Microsoft Applied Skills (agents in Microsoft Foundry), AWS Agentic AI Demonstrated |
| 2. Foundations exam | Speak the cloud buyer's language | AWS AI Practitioner (AIF-C01), Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901) or Google Generative AI Leader |
| 3. Builder exam | Validate hands-on generative AI skills | AWS Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01), Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate (AI-103) or Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate |
| 4. Platform specialty | Match your employer's stack | NVIDIA NCA-GENL, Salesforce Agentforce Specialist, Snowflake SnowPro Specialty: Gen AI, Claude certifications (partners) |

The [prep course](course/README.md) covers the hands-on content of stages 1 and 3. Pre-sales skills (discovery, demos, POCs) have no dominant certification; your capstone POC is the portfolio proof.
