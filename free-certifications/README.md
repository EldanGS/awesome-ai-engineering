# Free AI Certifications Tracker

> Your AI credentials, all in one place. A curated catalog of **free** AI courses from **FAANG, Big Tech and the frontier AI labs** that award a **free certificate or badge**, ordered as a study path for AI Solutions Engineers and applied AI engineers.

**132 credentials** from **14 providers**. Fork this repository and tick the boxes as you earn each credential, then add them to your LinkedIn *Licenses & certifications* section.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do the work yourself. Using bots or AI agents to complete courses, quizzes or exams on your behalf breaks most providers' terms, can get your account and credentials revoked, and defeats the purpose: interviewers test the skill, not the badge.

> [!NOTE]
> Generated from [`catalog.json`](catalog.json) by [`scripts/build_catalog.py`](../scripts/build_catalog.py). Free status is reported by the provider and can change; check the course page before you start. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any listed provider.

## How to add a credential

1. Confirm on the official page that both the course **and** the credential are free, and that the provider is a FAANG, Big Tech or frontier AI lab platform. Small training sites and resellers are not accepted.
2. Add an object to `catalog.json` with `title`, `provider`, `url`, `credential` (`certificate` or `badge`), `minutes` (or `null`) and one of the track names in `config.json`.
3. Run `python3 scripts/build_catalog.py` and commit both files.
